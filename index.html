Luiz A. Silva Lima

PhD · Postdoctoral Fellow

National Laboratory for Astrophysics (LNA)

I am a postdoctoral fellow in Astrophysics at the National Laboratory for Astrophysics (LNA) in Brazil, working primarily with integral-field spectroscopy of galaxies. My work combines stellar population analysis, kinematics and star formation history recovery with detailed studies of the ionised interstellar medium. I am a member of the GECKOS survey and an associate member of TIMER.
